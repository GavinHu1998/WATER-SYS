PRODUCT
Product name: PWS Stream Manual Water Distiller.
Product description: The PWS Stream is a manual water distiller intended for home use, a small office, or a family. It uses distillation combined with a high-capacity activated carbon filter to produce distilled water.

- Distilling capacity: 8.0 US gallons (30.0 L) per 24 hours under optimum conditions.
- The product page describes an 8 gallon/day boiling tank.
- Optional storage tank capacity: 3.0 US gallons (11.4 L).
- When manually filled, the distiller will distill approximately one gallon of pure water before automatically shutting off.
- Performance depends on conditions including water temperature, ambient temperature, line voltage, boiling tank cleanliness, and boiler fill level. Specifications may change without notice.

DISTILLATION PROCESS
- Raw or tap water is heated and brought to a boil.
- Pure steam rises from the boiling water.
- The steam enters a baffle system that directs the steam toward the cooling coil.
- As steam enters the cooling coil, a gas vent releases volatile gases from the coil.
- The cooling coil condenses the steam into water.
- The distilled water is combined with a high-capacity activated carbon filtration stage.

OPERATION
- The PWS Stream is manually filled.
- When manually filled, it distills approximately one gallon of pure water before automatically shutting off.
- The float switch shuts the distiller off when the water level is low.
